Behind the casket follows a loose double line of mourners dressed entirely in black - men in dark suits and hats, elderly women in black dresses with black lace veils, heads bowed, hands clasped. Nobody hurries. Nobody speaks. The spacing between figures stays even, like a slow-moving chain.
